About Me

I'm Márk, a 3D artist specialising in environments and hard-surface design for AAA game productions. I care about the details most players never notice — the way metal wears, how fog holds a streetlight, how light falls across a wall that has seen weather.

Every asset I build is made with a production mindset: clean topology, smart texture use and optimisation that survives contact with a real engine. The result is work that reads as both cinematic and technically reliable.
